The tested runtime binary is withheld because ESP-IDF compiles the Wi-Fi SSID and
password (from local_wifi_config.h) directly into the app image, and the password
is the one credential kept private.

Its identity is still fixed by hash for the evidence record:
  ESP_LOCAL_007_CONCURRENT.bin  SHA-256:
  6266f600f1d463b0d73878374fad178cae62497dbf38854e4df92a69a44b4abf

To reproduce: build firmware/ with your own local_wifi_config.h. A rebuild will not
match the hash above (compile-time stamps + a different Wi-Fi password), which is
expected — the source and sdkconfig are the reproducible artifacts, the hash is the
identity of the specific tested build.
