## Publication scope and preservation

The MD001 publication retains all 599 files from the November 21, 2026 staging selection without modification to their original contents.

The consolidated publication records are:

- [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md)
- [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md)
- [ORIGINAL_HASH_REFERENCES.tsv](ORIGINAL_HASH_REFERENCES.tsv)

The consolidated SHA-256 manifest covers the selected experimental evidence, implementation files, configurations, historical records, and supporting publication documentation.

Repository navigation READMEs, Git metadata, external review files, and the consolidated manifest itself are outside this checksum scope.

Publication-time checksums establish the integrity of the selected publication files. They do not constitute recovered collection-time checksums or establish retrospective authentication of experimental evidence.

All original manifests and historical checksum references remain unchanged. Historical coverage limitations, unavailable records, inherited discrepancies, and publication exclusions are documented in the following sections.

No original evidence, runtime implementation, or historical manifest was modified. No experiment was rerun during publication reconciliation.
